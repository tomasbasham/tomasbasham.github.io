{% comment %} A collapsible contents panel for a post that belongs to a series (`series: ` in its front matter). The members come from the `series_posts` filter in _plugins/series.rb; everything about how they look is decided here. The panel names the series using _data/series.yml, falling back to the key, says where this post sits in it, and starts closed. It is a native
element, so opening and closing it needs no script.
