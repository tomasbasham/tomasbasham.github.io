{% comment %} Applies a remembered light/dark choice before the first paint. Usage: {% include theme-init.html %} (in , before the stylesheets) Why: the store only runs once Alpine boots, which is after the page has painted. Without this, a visitor who chose light on a dark system would see a flash of dark first. Note: 'system' (or nothing stored) needs no attribute: Avanti follows prefers-color-scheme by default. The key must match THEME_STORAGE_KEY in _resources/js/stores/dark-mode.ts. {% endcomment %}
